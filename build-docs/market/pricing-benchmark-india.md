# DifAi — Market Pricing Benchmark (India)

**Date:** 5 Nov 2026 · **Market:** India only · **Currency:** ₹ per month unless stated
**Basis:** competitor prices from public vendor pages and comparison sites (Jul–Nov 2026). Recheck them before quoting to customers.

---

## 1. Summary
- **DifAi is the only Indian product that combines AI drafting with full practice management:** eCourts diary, eFiling, limitation, clients and billing.
- **Solo at ₹1,199/mo (yearly, incl. GST)** costs about the same as the two-app stack an advocate buys today (DraftBotPro + Advocate Diary ≈ ₹1,250), and it covers more.
- **Chamber is about 3–4× cheaper per advocate** than LawCentral Teams and DraftBotPro Chamber.
- **We sit in the mid band** (₹1,000–5,000), above budget drafting tools (₹399–1,099) and far below premium legal AI (₹9,000–17,000 per user).
- **Our free tier is the most generous in the market:** 30 drafts a month until 1 Jun 2027, then a free-forever diary.

## 6. Pricing rules (for sales)
- **Maximum discount:**
  - 20% off yearly for bar association members;
  - founder offer ₹999/mo for Solo yearly;
  - **no stacking** of discounts.
- **No price below ₹999/mo for Solo yearly.** It protects margin and positioning.
- **Yearly first:** always quote the yearly per-month price, with monthly as the alternative.
- **GST:** Solo and Student prices include GST; Chamber, Clerk and Firm prices are +18% GST (firms claim input credit).
- **Firm (9+) quotes:** ₹999–1,200 per advocate per month on yearly billing, with a minimum of 9 advocates.
- **Margin guardrail:** AI cost per paying user must stay below 35% of plan revenue, reviewed monthly from /admin AI cost.
