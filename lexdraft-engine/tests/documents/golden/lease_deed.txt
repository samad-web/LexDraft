LEASE DEED

THIS LEASE DEED is made and executed at Chennai on 2024-01-01 BETWEEN Sudarshan Kaur, W/o Paramjit Singh, R/o Chennai (hereinafter referred to as the LESSOR, which expression shall include his legal heirs, successors, representatives and assigns) of the FIRST PART; AND M/s Dave Thomson Associates (India) Pvt. Ltd., through its Director (hereinafter referred to as the LESSEE, which expression shall include its successors and assigns) of the SECOND PART.
WHEREAS the Lessor is the owner of the basement portion, admeasuring 760 sq.ft., at M-170, Anna Nagar, Chennai (hereinafter the demised premises) and is desirous of letting out the same; AND WHEREAS the Lessee has offered to take the demised premises on lease, and the Lessor has agreed to let out the same on the terms hereinafter specified.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS UNDER:
1. That the Lessor lets out the demised premises to the Lessee for a term of 24 months with effect from 2024-01-01, at a monthly rent of Rs. 4000/- (exclusive of electricity and water charges).

2. That the rent shall be escalated as follows: the rent shall be increased by 10% per annum after every 12 months.

3. That the Lessee has paid Rs. 36000/- as an interest-free security deposit, refundable on termination of the lease after adjustment of dues.
4. That either party may terminate this lease by giving 3 months' written notice.
5. That the Lessee shall pay the electricity and water charges, keep the premises in good tenantable condition, use them only for the authorised purpose, and deliver vacant possession on the expiry or termination of the lease.
6. That the Lessor shall pay all municipal and property taxes, and shall not interfere with the Lessee's peaceful enjoyment of the demised premises.
