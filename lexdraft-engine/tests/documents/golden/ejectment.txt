PLAINT FILED UNDER ORDER VII RULE 1 OF THE CODE OF CIVIL PROCEDURE, 1908 FOR EJECTMENT AND DAMAGES FOR WRONGFUL USE AND OCCUPATION

MOST RESPECTFULLY SHOWETH:
1. That the plaintiff(s), being the owner(s) of Flat No. 7, Nehru Place Towers, Chennai, let out the said premises to the defendant vide unregistered lease deed dated 01-04-2020.
2. That the said lease expired on 2023-03-31, whereafter the defendant became a month-to-month tenant of the plaintiff(s). Under Section 106 of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882, as amended in 2002, a tenancy from month to month is terminable by fifteen days' notice; the notice need not expire with the end of a month of the tenancy.
3. That the plaintiff(s) served upon the defendant a notice under s.106 of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882 (notice dated 20-11-2023) terminating the tenancy with effect from 2023-12-31.
4. That the defendant received the said notice on 2023-11-25, i.e. clear 36 days before the date of termination, and it is therefore a valid notice under the Transfer of Property Act.
5. That the defendant, having neither vacated the premises nor shown any intention to vacate, is in wrongful use and occupation since 2024-01-01, and is liable to pay damages for use and occupation at Rs. 1000/- per day, the prevailing rate for such premises. The damages up to the date of the plaint are: Rs. 1,000 per day from 01.01.2024 to 01.03.2024 (60 days) = Rs. 60,000.


6. That the suit premises are outside the Tamil Nadu Regulation of Rights and Responsibilities of Landlords and Tenants Act, 2017 [reason the Act does not apply to be stated], and this Hon'ble Court, as the ordinary civil court, has jurisdiction to try the suit under Section 9 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908.

7. That the cause of action arose on 2024-01-01 at Chennai and is a continuing one.
8. That this Hon'ble Court has jurisdiction: the premises are situated within the territorial jurisdiction of this Court.
9. That the suit is valued for the relief of ejectment under Section 50 of the Tamil Nadu Court Fees and Suits Valuation Act, 1955 on the rent payable for the year next before the date of presenting the plaint, Rs. 81696/-, and for the damages for use and occupation up to the date of the plaint at the amount claimed, Rs. 60000/-, in all Rs. 141696/-, on which a court fee of Rs. 6000/- is paid; future damages will be valued and the fee on them paid when they are ascertained under Order XX Rule 12 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, and the plaintiff(s) undertake to pay any additional court fee found due.

PRAYER:
It is, therefore, most respectfully prayed that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to:
(a) pass a decree for ejectment against the defendant and in favour of the plaintiff(s);

(b) pass a decree for Rs. 60000/- towards damages for use and occupation from 2024-01-01 to the date of the plaint;
(c) direct an enquiry into, and pass a decree for, future damages for use and occupation at Rs. 1000/- per day from the date of the plaint till the date of delivery of vacant possession (Order XX Rule 12 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908);
(d) award the costs of the suit; and
(e) pass such further or other orders as this Hon'ble Court may deem fit and proper in the circumstances of the case, and thus render justice.
