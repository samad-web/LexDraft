DEED OF DISSOLUTION OF PARTNERSHIP

THIS DEED OF DISSOLUTION is executed at Chennai on 2024-01-01 BETWEEN:

   1. A, S/o X, R/o Chennai;

   2. B, S/o Y, R/o Chennai;

(hereinafter collectively referred to as the PARTNERS).
WHEREAS the parties have been carrying on business in partnership under the firm M/s ABC on the basis of the partnership deed dated 01-01-2020; AND WHEREAS the parties have mutually decided to dissolve the partnership and to reduce the terms of dissolution into writing.

NOW, THEREFORE, THIS DEED WITNESSES AS UNDER:
1. That the partnership constituted under the partnership deed dated 01-01-2020, carried on under the firm M/s ABC, stands dissolved with effect from 2024-01-01.

2. That all the accounts of the partnership have been settled, and all trading results, profits, losses and balances have been checked and accepted by the parties as per the account books.

3. That all records and account books of the dissolved partnership have been delivered to the First Party, who shall notify all concerned authorities of the dissolution.
4. That the partners shall be liable for their individual taxes; and each party releases and discharges the other from all actions, claims and demands on account of the said partnership.
5. That any dispute arising in relation to this deed shall be referred to a sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties hereto have signed this deed on the date and at the place first above written, in the presence of the following witnesses.

FIRST PARTNER
SECOND PARTNER

   2. Witness B
