DEED OF DISSOLUTION OF PARTNERSHIP
THIS DEED OF DISSOLUTION OF PARTNERSHIP is made and executed at Chennai on 2024-01-01 BETWEEN:

   1. A, S/o X, R/o Chennai;

   2. B, S/o Y, R/o Chennai;

(hereinafter collectively called the "PARTNERS", which expression shall include their respective heirs, executors, administrators and legal representatives).

WHEREAS the Partners have been carrying on business in partnership under the name and style of M/s ABC (hereinafter called the "Firm"), constituted under the partnership deed dated 01-01-2020 sharing the profits and losses in equal shares;
AND WHEREAS the Partners have mutually agreed to dissolve the Firm on the terms hereinafter set out;

NOW THIS DEED OF DISSOLUTION WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:
1. DISSOLUTION: The partnership constituted under the partnership deed dated 01-01-2020 and carried on under the name M/s ABC stands dissolved with effect from 2024-01-01.

2. ACCOUNTS: The accounts of the Firm have been taken and settled up to the date of dissolution, and have been checked and accepted by all the Partners.




6. RECORDS: The books of account and records of the Firm shall remain with the First Party, who shall make them available to the other Partners for inspection when reasonably required.
7. PUBLIC NOTICE: The Partners shall give public notice of the dissolution as required by the Indian Partnership Act, 1932, and, if the Firm is registered, intimate the Registrar of Firms of the dissolution.
8. RELEASE: Subject to the terms of this deed, each Partner releases and discharges the others from all claims and demands in respect of the Firm, and each Partner shall be liable for his or her individual taxes.
9. DISPUTES: Any dispute arising in relation to this deed shall be referred to a sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
