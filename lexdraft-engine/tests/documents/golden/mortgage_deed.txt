DEED OF SIMPLE MORTGAGE
THIS DEED OF SIMPLE MORTGAGE is made and executed at Chennai on 2024-01-01 BY
A, S/o X, R/o Chennai (hereinafter called the "MORTGAGOR", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include his/her heirs, executors, administrators, legal representatives and assigns) of the ONE PART;
IN FAVOUR OF
M/s ABC Ltd., a company incorporated under the Companies Act (hereinafter called the "MORTGAGEE", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include its successors-in-interest and assigns) of the OTHER PART.

WHEREAS the property more fully described in the Schedule hereunder (hereinafter called the "Mortgaged Property") absolutely belongs to the Mortgagor, whose title thereto is as follows: [mortgagor's title (prior deed) to be provided];

AND WHEREAS the Mortgagor wants to construct a residential building on the said plot;

AND WHEREAS the Mortgagor requested the Mortgagee for a loan of Rs. 200000/- (Rupees two lakh only), and the Mortgagee agreed to advance the same on the security of the Mortgaged Property by way of simple mortgage, on the terms hereinafter set out;

NOW THIS DEED OF MORTGAGE WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:
1. LOAN AND REPAYMENT: In consideration of the sum of Rs. 200000/- (Rupees two lakh only) advanced by the Mortgagee to the Mortgagor, the receipt of which the Mortgagor hereby acknowledges, the Mortgagor covenants to repay the said principal sum together with simple interest at 12.0% per annum, as follows: in equal monthly instalments of Rs. 2000/-.
2. PERSONAL COVENANT: The Mortgagor hereby binds him/herself personally to pay the mortgage money and interest in accordance with clause 1.
3. MORTGAGE: As security for the repayment of the mortgage money and interest, the Mortgagor hereby mortgages the Mortgaged Property to the Mortgagee by way of simple mortgage, without delivering possession of it, and agrees that, in the event of default in payment, the Mortgagee shall have the right to cause the Mortgaged Property to be sold and the proceeds of sale to be applied, so far as may be necessary, in payment of the mortgage money (s.58(b), Transfer of Property Act, 1882).
4. REMEDY ON DEFAULT: In the event of default, the Mortgagee's remedy shall be to sue for the sale of the Mortgaged Property through the Court and to recover the amount due. Nothing in this deed authorises the Mortgagee to sell the Mortgaged Property without the intervention of the Court.
5. REDEMPTION: The Mortgagor may repay the entire mortgage money with interest at any time within the period stated in clause 1. On such payment the Mortgagee shall acknowledge full discharge in writing, execute a deed of release of this mortgage at the Mortgagor's cost, and return any title documents of the Mortgagor held by it (right of redemption, s.60, Transfer of Property Act, 1882).
6. POSSESSION AND PRESERVATION: The Mortgagor shall remain in possession of the Mortgaged Property and shall, so long as any amount remains due, pay all taxes and charges on it, commit no waste, and not sell, mortgage, lease or otherwise encumber it without the written consent of the Mortgagee.
7. TITLE: The Mortgagor covenants that he/she has good and marketable title to the Mortgaged Property, and that it is free from all encumbrances, charges, attachments and court proceedings.
