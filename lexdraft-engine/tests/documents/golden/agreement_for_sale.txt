AGREEMENT FOR SALE
THIS AGREEMENT FOR SALE is made and executed at Chennai on 2024-01-01 BETWEEN
A, S/o X, R/o Chennai (hereinafter called the "VENDOR", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include his/her heirs, executors, administrators, legal representatives and assigns) of the ONE PART;
AND
B, S/o Y, R/o Chennai (hereinafter called the "PURCHASER", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include his/her heirs, executors, administrators, legal representatives and assigns) of the OTHER PART.

WHEREAS the Vendor is the absolute owner in possession of the property more fully described in the Schedule hereunder (hereinafter called the "Schedule Property");
AND WHEREAS the Vendor has agreed to sell, and the Purchaser has agreed to purchase, the Schedule Property for a total sale consideration of Rs. 5000000/- (Rupees fifty lakh only), on the terms and conditions hereinafter set out;

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AND IT IS HEREBY AGREED BETWEEN THE PARTIES AS FOLLOWS:
1. PRICE: The Vendor agrees to sell, and the Purchaser agrees to purchase, the Schedule Property free from all encumbrances for a total sale consideration of Rs. 5000000/- (Rupees fifty lakh only).
2. ADVANCE: The Purchaser has this day paid to the Vendor Rs. 500000/- (Rupees five lakh only) as advance and part of the sale consideration by cheque No. 010806 drawn on Indian Bank, the receipt of which the Vendor hereby acknowledges.
3. BALANCE: The balance sale consideration of Rs. 4500000/- (Rupees forty-five lakh only) shall be paid by the Purchaser to the Vendor within 6 months from the date hereof, at the time of execution and registration of the sale deed.
4. TITLE DOCUMENTS: The Vendor shall deliver to the Purchaser, for scrutiny, the original title deeds of the Schedule Property and an up-to-date encumbrance certificate, and shall furnish such other documents as may reasonably be required to verify the title.
5. TITLE AND NON-ALIENATION: The Vendor shall make out a good and marketable title to the Schedule Property, free from all encumbrances, and shall not, while this agreement subsists, sell, mortgage, lease or otherwise encumber the Schedule Property or enter into any agreement concerning it with any other person.
6. SALE DEED AND POSSESSION: On receipt of the balance sale consideration, the Vendor shall execute a sale deed in favour of the Purchaser, present it for registration, and deliver vacant possession of the Schedule Property to the Purchaser.
7. DEFAULT: If the Vendor fails to make out a marketable title or to perform his/her part of this agreement, the Purchaser shall be entitled to enforce specific performance of this agreement or, at his/her option, to the refund of the advance paid. If the Purchaser fails to pay the balance sale consideration within 6 months from the date hereof, the Vendor may, after notice in writing, terminate this agreement.
8. EXPENSES: The stamp duty, registration charges and other expenses of the sale deed shall be borne by the Purchaser.
