AGREEMENT FOR SALE

THIS AGREEMENT FOR SALE is made at Chennai on 2024-01-01 BETWEEN A, S/o X, R/o Chennai (hereinafter referred to as the VENDOR, which expression shall include his heirs, executors, administrators and assigns) of the FIRST PART; AND B, S/o Y, R/o Chennai (hereinafter referred to as the VENDEE/PURCHASER, which expression shall include his heirs, executors, administrators and assigns) of the SECOND PART.
WHEREAS the Vendor is the absolute owner of Plot No. 45, admeasuring 2400 sq.ft., Anna Nagar, Chennai (hereinafter referred to as the said property).
AND WHEREAS the Vendor has agreed to sell the said property to the Vendee at the price and on the conditions mentioned hereinafter.

NOW IT IS AGREED BETWEEN THE PARTIES AS FOLLOWS:
1. That the Vendor agrees to sell, transfer and convey the said property to the Vendee for a total consideration of Rs. 5000000/-.
2. That the Vendee has paid Rs. 500000/- as earnest money (by cheque No. 010806 drawn on Indian Bank), the receipt of which the Vendor acknowledges; the balance shall be paid at the time of the final sale deed.
3. That the Vendor shall make out a marketable title to the said property, free from encumbrances and reasonable doubts.
4. That the sale shall be completed within 6 months from the date hereof.
5. That the expenses of stamp duty, registration charges and all incidental expenses for the agreement and the sale deed shall be borne by the Vendee.
6. That if the Vendor fails to make out a clear marketable title, the Vendee may cancel this agreement on fifteen days' notice, and the Vendor shall return the earnest money; if the Vendee fails to perform his obligations, the Vendor may cancel on fifteen days' notice and forfeit the earnest money.
