GIFT DEED
THIS DEED OF GIFT is made and executed at Chennai on 2024-01-01 BY
XYZ, S/o ABC, R/o Chennai (hereinafter called the "DONOR", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include his/her heirs, executors, administrators, legal representatives and assigns) of the ONE PART;
IN FAVOUR OF
PQR, S/o XYZ, R/o Chennai (hereinafter called the "DONEE", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include his/her heirs, executors, administrators, legal representatives and assigns) of the OTHER PART.

WHEREAS the property more fully described in the Schedule hereunder (hereinafter called the "Schedule Property") absolutely belongs to the Donor, whose title thereto is as follows: the Donor is the owner of the said property, having purchased it vide registered sale deed dated 01-01-2010;
AND WHEREAS the Schedule Property is free from all encumbrances, and the Donor is fully entitled to dispose of the same;
AND WHEREAS the Donee is the Donor's son, and the Donor, out of natural love and affection for the Donee, is desirous of gifting the Schedule Property to the Donee without any consideration;

NOW THIS DEED OF GIFT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:
1. GIFT: Out of natural love and affection, and without any monetary consideration, the Donor hereby voluntarily and irrevocably gifts, transfers and conveys the Schedule Property unto the Donee, TO HAVE AND TO HOLD the same absolutely and forever.
2. ACCEPTANCE: The Donee hereby accepts the gift during the lifetime of the Donor, and in token of such acceptance has signed this deed (s.122, Transfer of Property Act, 1882).
3. POSSESSION: The Donor has this day delivered possession of the Schedule Property to the Donee, who shall hereafter hold and enjoy it as absolute owner, with full rights of alienation.
4. NO RESERVATION: The Donor has retained no right, title, interest or lien in the Schedule Property, and neither the Donor nor any person claiming through him/her shall have any claim to it hereafter.
5. TITLE: The Donor covenants that he/she has good title to the Schedule Property and that it is free from encumbrances.
6. MUTATION: The Donee shall be entitled to have the patta, revenue records, property tax assessment and service connections transferred to his/her name on the strength of this deed, and the Donor shall sign all papers required for the purpose.
7. VALUE: The market value of the Schedule Property is Rs. 5000000/- (Rupees fifty lakh only).
