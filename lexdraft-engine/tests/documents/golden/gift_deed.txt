GIFT DEED

THIS GIFT DEED is made and executed at Chennai on 2024-01-01 BETWEEN XYZ, S/o ABC, R/o Chennai (hereinafter called the DONOR) of the ONE PART; AND PQR, S/o XYZ, R/o Chennai (hereinafter called the DONEE) of the OTHER PART. The expressions DONOR and DONEE shall include their respective heirs, successors, executors, nominees, assignees and legal representatives.
AND WHEREAS the Donor is the owner of the said property, having purchased it vide registered sale deed dated 01-01-2010
AND WHEREAS the said property is free from all encumbrances, and the Donor is fully entitled to dispose of the same.
AND WHEREAS the Donor has great love and affection for the Donee, being his/her son, and has agreed to gift, without any monetary consideration, the property described in the Schedule; and the Donee has agreed to accept the same.

NOW THIS GIFT DEED WITNESSETH AS UNDER:
1. That the Donor hereby gifts the residential house bearing No. 13, Anna Nagar, Chennai unto the Donee on account of natural love and affection.
2. That the possession of the gifted property has been handed over and delivered to the Donee by the Donor.
3. That the Donee has become the absolute and exclusive owner of the gifted property from today, with all rights of ownership therein.
4. That the Donor has been left with no right, title, interest or lien whatsoever in the gifted property henceforth.
5. That the market value of the gifted property is assessed at Rs. 5000000/-.
6. That the Donee shall get the gifted property mutated into his/her name in the revenue records on the basis of this deed, for which the Donor conveys his No Objection.

   Boundaries — East: Road; West: Park; North: House No. 12; South: House No. 14.
