SALE DEED
THIS DEED OF ABSOLUTE SALE is made and executed at Chennai on 2024-01-01 BY
A, S/o X, R/o Chennai (hereinafter called the "VENDOR", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include his/her heirs, executors, administrators, legal representatives and assigns) of the ONE PART;
IN FAVOUR OF
B, S/o Y, R/o Chennai (hereinafter called the "PURCHASER", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include his/her heirs, executors, administrators, legal representatives and assigns) of the OTHER PART.

WHEREAS the property more fully described in the Schedule hereunder (hereinafter called the "Schedule Property") absolutely belongs to the Vendor, whose title thereto is as follows: the Vendor purchased the said plot vide sale deed dated 06-08-2015 registered as document No. 5560;
AND WHEREAS the Vendor has since been in absolute possession and enjoyment of the Schedule Property, and has full right and power to convey the same;
AND WHEREAS the Vendor offered to sell, and the Purchaser agreed to purchase, the Schedule Property for a total sale consideration of Rs. 2500000/- (Rupees twenty-five lakh only);

NOW THIS DEED OF SALE WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:
1. CONSIDERATION: The total sale consideration of Rs. 2500000/- (Rupees twenty-five lakh only) has been paid by the Purchaser to the Vendor in full, as follows: (a) Rs. 2000000/- (Rupees twenty lakh only) paid as advance; and (b) the balance of Rs. 500000/- (Rupees five lakh only) paid this day by cheque No. 010806 dated 29-09-2024 drawn on Indian Bank. The Vendor hereby admits and acknowledges the receipt of the entire sale consideration of Rs. 2500000/-, and nothing remains due from the Purchaser.
2. CONVEYANCE: In consideration of the said sum so paid, the Vendor hereby sells, conveys, transfers and assigns unto the Purchaser the Schedule Property, together with all easements, rights of way and appurtenances, and all the right, title, interest, claim and demand of the Vendor therein, TO HAVE AND TO HOLD the same unto the Purchaser absolutely and forever.
3. POSSESSION: The Vendor has this day delivered vacant physical possession of the Schedule Property to the Purchaser, who shall hereafter hold and enjoy the same as absolute owner, with full rights of alienation, without any let or hindrance by the Vendor or any person claiming through him/her.
4. TITLE: The Vendor covenants that he/she has good and marketable title to the Schedule Property, and that it is free from all encumbrances, charges, mortgages, liens, attachments, court proceedings, prior agreements and acquisition proceedings.
5. INDEMNITY: If the Purchaser's title or possession is disturbed by reason of any defect in the Vendor's title, or by any claim of a person claiming through the Vendor, the Vendor shall make good all loss and expense suffered by the Purchaser, and his/her other properties shall be liable for the same.
6. TITLE DOCUMENTS: The Vendor shall deliver to the Purchaser all documents of title relating to the Schedule Property in his/her possession or power.
7. MUTATION: The Purchaser shall be entitled to have the patta, revenue records, property tax assessment and service connections, if any, transferred to his/her name, and the Vendor shall sign all papers required for the purpose.
8. TAXES: All taxes and charges in respect of the Schedule Property up to the date of this deed have been paid by the Vendor, and shall hereafter be paid by the Purchaser.
9. FURTHER ASSURANCE: The Vendor shall, at the request and cost of the Purchaser, execute and do all such further acts, deeds and things as may reasonably be required for more perfectly assuring the Schedule Property to the Purchaser.
