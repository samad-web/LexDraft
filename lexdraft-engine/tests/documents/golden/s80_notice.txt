Under instructions from my client Sri Kumar, I hereby give you notice under s.80 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, and state that my client intends to sue Government of Tamil Nadu, Education Department, Chennai after the expiry of two months from the service of this notice, unless the reliefs claimed below are granted within the said period. The particulars required by s.80 are:
(1) Name and description of the plaintiff: Sri Kumar, S/o Rajan, an employee of the Education Department, residing at Chennai.
(2) Cause of action: the plaintiff was illegally dismissed from service with effect from 01-06-2023. The said act is illegal: [ground on which the act is illegal to be provided].
(3) Reliefs claimed:

   (a) reinstatement of the plaintiff in service

   (b) recovery of salary for the period of dismissal

My client therefore calls upon you to grant the above reliefs within two months from the date of service of this notice, failing which my client will institute a suit against Government of Tamil Nadu, Education Department, Chennai for the said reliefs, with costs, without further reference to you.
