DEED OF RELEASE (RELINQUISHMENT)
THIS DEED OF RELEASE (RELINQUISHMENT) is made and executed at Chennai on 2024-01-01 BY

   1. Avadh, D/o Late X, R/o Chennai;

   2. Bala, D/o Late X, R/o Chennai;

   3. Devilal, S/o Late X, R/o Chennai;

(hereinafter called the "RELEASORS", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include their respective heirs, executors, administrators, legal representatives and assigns) of the ONE PART;
IN FAVOUR OF
Ragini, W/o Late X, R/o Chennai (hereinafter called the "RELEASEE", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include his/her heirs, executors, administrators, legal representatives and assigns) of the OTHER PART.

WHEREAS the property more fully described in the Schedule hereunder (hereinafter called the "Schedule Property") belonged absolutely to the late X;
AND WHEREAS the said X died on [date of death to be provided], and on his/her death the Releasors and the Releasee, as his/her legal heirs, became entitled to undivided shares in the Schedule Property by inheritance;
AND WHEREAS the Releasors are entitled to 4/5th share in the Schedule Property, and are desirous of releasing and relinquishing the same in favour of the Releasee, out of natural love and affection and without any consideration;

NOW THIS DEED OF RELEASE WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:
1. RELEASE: The Releasors, of their own free will and without any consideration, hereby release and relinquish unto the Releasee all their right, title, interest, claim and demand to the extent of 4/5th share in the Schedule Property, TO HOLD the same unto the Releasee absolutely.
2. NO CLAIM: Henceforth the Releasors and their heirs and legal representatives shall have no right, title, interest or claim whatsoever in the share hereby released, and the Releasee shall hold and enjoy the same as absolute owner.
3. TITLE: The Releasors covenant that the share hereby released is free from encumbrances, and that they have not dealt with it in any manner.
4. MUTATION: The Releasee shall be entitled to have the patta and revenue records mutated to reflect the share released, and the Releasors shall sign all papers required for the purpose.
