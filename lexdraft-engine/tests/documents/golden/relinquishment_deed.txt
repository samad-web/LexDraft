DEED OF RELINQUISHMENT

THIS DEED OF RELINQUISHMENT is executed at Chennai on 2024-01-01 BY:

   1. Avadh, D/o Late X, R/o Chennai;

   2. Bala, D/o Late X, R/o Chennai;

   3. Devilal, S/o Late X, R/o Chennai;

(hereinafter collectively called the RELEASORS, which expression shall include their heirs, successors, legal representatives and executors) of the FIRST PART; IN FAVOUR OF Ragini, W/o Late X, R/o Chennai (hereinafter called the RELEASEE, which expression shall include her heirs, successors, legal representatives and executors) of the SECOND PART.
WHEREAS the late X was the sole and absolute owner of the double-storey house built over 200 sq.yds at Chennai; AND WHEREAS, on his death, the Releasors and the Releasee became co-owners of the said property by inheritance; AND WHEREAS the Releasors are desirous of giving up their 4/5th share in the said property in favour of the Releasee, on account of natural love and affection and without any consideration.

NOW THIS DEED OF RELINQUISHMENT WITNESSES AS UNDER:
1. That the Releasors voluntarily, and of their own free will, give up and release all their right, title and interest to the extent of their 4/5th share in the double-storey house built over 200 sq.yds at Chennai in favour of the Releasee, who now becomes the sole and absolute owner of the said property.
2. That the Releasors and their heirs are left with no claim, title or interest in the property hereby relinquished.
3. That the possession of the said property is exclusively with the Releasee, who is entitled to have the property mutated and transferred into her name on the basis of this deed.

SCHEDULE OF PROPERTY:
the double-storey house built over 200 sq.yds at Chennai
