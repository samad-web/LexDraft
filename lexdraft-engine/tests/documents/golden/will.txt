WILL

THIS IS THE LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT of me, Rajan, S/o Late Krishnan, R/o 13 Gandhi Street, Adyar, Chennai, made at Chennai on 2024-01-01.
That life is uncertain, and this is my last Will by which I bequeath voluntarily, out of my own free will and in a sound state of mind, my self-acquired properties to the beneficiaries described hereunder.

I hereby revoke all former wills, codicils and testamentary dispositions made by me.
