CONTEMPT PETITION FILED UNDER SECTIONS 10 TO 12 OF THE CONTEMPT OF COURTS ACT, 1971

MOST RESPECTFULLY SHOWETH:
1. That in Civil Writ Petition No. 2420 of 2023 this Hon'ble Court, on 2023-10-01, passed an order granting status quo and restraining the respondents from demolishing the construction.
2. That the respondents/contemnors, in spite of the service and knowledge of the said order dated 2023-10-01, demolished the construction raised on the petitioners' plots on 2024-01-04.
3. That the respondents/contemnors have thereby knowingly and wilfully disobeyed the order of this Hon'ble Court and committed civil contempt within the meaning of Section 2(b) of the Contempt of Courts Act, 1971.
4. That the present petition is within one year of the date on which the contempt was committed (s.20 of the Contempt of Courts Act, 1971).
5. That the cause of action arose when the contemnors, having been apprised of the order, failed to comply, and is a continuing one.

PRAYER:
It is therefore prayed that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to punish the respondents/contemnors under Sections 10 to 12 of the Contempt of Courts Act, 1971 for wilful disobedience of the order dated 2023-10-01 in Civil Writ Petition No. 2420 of 2023, and direct the respondents to comply with the said order forthwith, and pass such further or other orders as this Hon'ble Court may deem fit and proper in the circumstances of the case, and thus render justice.
