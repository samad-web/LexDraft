CONTEMPT PETITION UNDER SECTIONS 11 AND 12 OF THE CONTEMPT OF COURTS ACT, 1971

MOST RESPECTFULLY SHOWETH:
1. That in Civil Writ Petition No. 2420 of 2023 this Hon'ble Court, on 2023-10-01, passed an order granting status quo and restraining the respondents from demolishing the construction.
2. That the respondents/contemnors, in spite of the service and knowledge of the said order dated 2023-10-01, demolished the construction raised on the petitioners' plots on 2024-01-04.
3. That the respondents/contemnors have thereby knowingly and wilfully disobeyed the order of this Hon'ble Court and committed contempt of court.
4. That the present petition is within one year of the date on which the contempt was committed (s.20 of the Contempt of Courts Act, 1971).
5. That the cause of action arose when the contemnors, having been apprised of the order, refused to comply, and is a continuing one.

PRAYER:
It is, therefore, most respectfully prayed that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to:
(a) initiate contempt proceedings against the above-named contemnors and punish them in accordance with law; and
(b) pass such other and further order(s) as may be deemed fit and proper on the facts and in the circumstances of this case.
