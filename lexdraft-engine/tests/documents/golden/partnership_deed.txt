DEED OF PARTNERSHIP

THIS DEED OF PARTNERSHIP is executed at Chennai on 2024-01-01 BETWEEN:

   1. X, S/o A, R/o Chennai (share: 51%);

   2. Y, S/o B, R/o Chennai (share: 49%);

(hereinafter collectively referred to as the PARTNERS, which expression shall include their respective heirs, successors, executors and legal representatives).
WHEREAS the parties have agreed to commence and carry on business in partnership on the terms set out hereunder.

NOW, THEREFORE, THIS DEED WITNESSES AS UNDER:
1. That the name and style of the partnership shall be M/s XYZ.
2. That the business of the partnership shall be interior decoration, furnishing and the manufacture and sale of furniture.
3. That the business shall be deemed to have commenced on 2024-01-01, and its principal place of business shall be at E-1 Ram Nagar, Chennai.
4. That the shares of the partners in the profits and losses shall be as follows:

   - X: 51%

   - Y: 49%

5. CAPITAL: the initial capital of Rs. 15,000/- has been contributed by each partner.
6. That the partnership shall maintain regular books of account at its principal place of business, and its accounting year shall run from 1st April to 31st March.

7. That the partnership shall be a partnership at will; any partner intending to retire or dissolve the firm shall give fifteen days' advance notice to the other partners.

8. That any dispute between the partners shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996; and in all other matters not provided herein, the partnership shall be governed by the Indian Partnership Act, 1932.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties hereto have signed this deed on the date and at the place first above written, in the presence of the following witnesses.

FIRST PARTNER
SECOND PARTNER

WITNESSES:

   1. Witness A

   2. Witness B
