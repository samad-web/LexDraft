DEED OF PARTNERSHIP
THIS DEED OF PARTNERSHIP is made and executed at Chennai on 2024-01-01 BETWEEN:

   1. X, S/o A, R/o Chennai;

   2. Y, S/o B, R/o Chennai;

(hereinafter collectively called the "PARTNERS", which expression shall include their respective heirs, executors, administrators and legal representatives).

WHEREAS the Partners have agreed to carry on business in partnership on the terms and conditions hereinafter set out, and to reduce those terms into writing;

NOW THIS DEED OF PARTNERSHIP WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:
1. NAME: The business shall be carried on in the name and style of M/s XYZ (hereinafter called the "Firm").
2. BUSINESS: The business of the Firm shall be interior decoration, furnishing and the manufacture and sale of furniture, and such other business as the Partners may mutually agree upon from time to time.
3. PLACE OF BUSINESS: The principal place of business of the Firm shall be at E-1 Ram Nagar, Chennai, or at such other place as the Partners may mutually decide.
4. COMMENCEMENT AND DURATION: The partnership shall commence on 2024-01-01. The partnership shall be a partnership at will, and any partner may dissolve the Firm by giving notice in writing to all the other partners of the intention to dissolve it (s.43, Indian Partnership Act, 1932).
5. CAPITAL: the initial capital of Rs. 15,000/- has been contributed by each partner

6. PROFIT AND LOSS: The net profits and losses of the Firm, after deducting all expenses including the remuneration payable to the working partners, shall be shared by the Partners as follows:

   - X: 51%

   - Y: 49%



9. BOOKS OF ACCOUNT: Proper books of account shall be kept at the principal place of business, and every partner shall have access to them and may inspect and copy them (s.12(d), Indian Partnership Act, 1932). The accounts shall be closed on 31st March of every year and the profit or loss ascertained.
10. DUTIES: Every partner shall be just and faithful to the others and carry on the business of the Firm to the greatest common advantage (s.9, Indian Partnership Act, 1932), and no partner shall, without the written consent of the others, stand surety, borrow or lend money on behalf of the Firm, or carry on any business competing with that of the Firm.
11. DISPUTES: Any dispute between the Partners touching the Firm shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
12. In all matters not provided for in this deed, the Indian Partnership Act, 1932 shall apply.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the Partners have signed this deed on the date and at the place first above written, in the presence of the following witnesses.


X (PARTNER 1)

Y (PARTNER 2)


WITNESSES (name, parentage and address):

   1. Witness A

   2. Witness B
